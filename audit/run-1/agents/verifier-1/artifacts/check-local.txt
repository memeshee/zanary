--- what this settles (all re-established by the verifier, not read from the hunter's artifact) ---
1. With the documented grant shape (empty allowlist = every receiver allowed, cap 10,000,000 zat,
   budget 50,000,000 zat, unexpired, not revoked) and an amount of 5,000,000 zat, payer::decide
   returns allowed=true for an invoice whose receiver is the text "not-an-address", and for one
   whose memo is 600 bytes — decide validates revoked/expiry/allowlist/cap/budget only, never the
   receiver's address form or the memo length.
2. Address::decode("not-an-address") returns None, so the payment loop's first fallible step
   (payer.rs:298) aborts the whole run for that invoice; MemoBytes::from_bytes(600) fails with
   "Memo length 600 is larger than maximum of 512" at payer.rs:304-305 (512 bytes is accepted).
3. The loop's first wallet-backed step cannot succeed offline: ops::propose_shielded_payment on an
   unsynced sandbox wallet fails with "could not propose the payment: Must scan blocks first".
   This is why the partial run (iteration 1 broadcasts, iteration k aborts) cannot be reproduced
   without the regtest rig (funded synced wallet + node/indexer).

Not established here (nor locally possible): that a broadcast payment from iteration 1 is mined
while no ledger/run/report artifact is written afterwards, and the re-run re-payment.
