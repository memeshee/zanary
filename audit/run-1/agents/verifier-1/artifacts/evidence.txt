What was re-established independently (every decisive claim):
1. The decide loop (payer.rs:246-270) prints ALLOW/REFUSE for every invoice and validates only
   revoked/expiry/allowlist/cap/budget; the invoice intake is plain serde (payer.rs:511-518).
   Observed both as a unit call and through the real CLI: an invoice whose receiver is
   "not-an-address" (amount 5,000,000, empty-allowlist grant, cap 10,000,000, budget 50,000,000)
   is decided allowed=true.
2. The payment loop (payer.rs:291-363) propagates every step with `?`: Address::decode (298-303),
   MemoBytes::from_bytes (304-305), propose_shielded_payment (306-307), build_sign_broadcast (308,
   which really does send_transaction to the indexer at ops.rs:407-415), mine_and_resync (309),
   mined_height_of (310). Address::decode("not-an-address") returns None; a 600-byte memo is
   admitted by decide and rejected by MemoBytes::from_bytes ("Memo length 600 is larger than
   maximum of 512"; 512 accepted).
3. ledger.jsonl and the run/report artifacts are written only after the loop (payer.rs:406-411:
   the two fs writes are 406 create_dir_all / 409 fs::write + 411 write_run_artifacts), and
   --expect-refused even later (469-478). No other write to --out exists in payer.rs, there is no
   Drop/panic path that records anything, and no Rust path anywhere reads a previous ledger
   (only demo shell/python summaries display it).
4. Observed through the real CLI in the sandbox: both invoices ALLOW (including the bad receiver),
   then the run aborts non-zero (exit 1) and --out is never created — no ledger.jsonl, no
   run.json, no report.json. (The abort sat at the node-RPC bootstrap, payer.rs:276, because the
   loop itself needs the rig; the ordering consequence — every post-decision failure loses the
   whole ledger — is what this shows.)
5. payer.rs contains no "error:" string; its only refusal reasons are decide()'s grant-rule
   strings, so the payer can never produce the "error:" refusal that payer_policy.rs:66-74
   exists to flag; and on a mid-loop abort no report is written for the property to read anyway.
6. docs/BOUNDED-PAYER.md:50 ("Every invoice produces exactly one ledger line — paid or refused")
   and :28 (budget checked per run, in ledger order) are the documented contract this breaks;
   `spent` starts at 0 on every invocation (payer.rs:247).
